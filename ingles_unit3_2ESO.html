📝 Resumen

  • Adjectives: tiny, unique, wonderful, boring, trendy, old-fashioned…
  • Opposites: rich/poor · huge/tiny · boring/interesting · old-fashioned/trendy
  • Past Simple (+): verb+-ed o forma irregular. E.g.: climbed, wrote, ran
  • Past Simple (-): didn't + infinitive. E.g.: didn't compete
  • Past Simple (?): Did + subject + infinitive?
  • There was/were: hechos pasados en singular/plural.
  • Used to: hábitos pasados ya no vigentes. E.g.: He used to work at night.
